    How it works

    What goes in

    • Recent form: last 5 and 10 games, season averages, and advanced stats like usage rate and true shooting.
    • Players out: the minutes, points and shots of injured teammates and opponents, from the ESPN injury report.
    • Matchup: the opponent's pace and defense over its last 10 games, and the player's history against that team.
    • Context: home or away, days of rest, back-to-backs.

    How accurate it is

    Trained on every game from 2015-16 to 2024-25, then tested on all 28,062 player-games of 2025-26. Average miss per game (lower is better):

    PTSREBAST
    Season average4.711.931.37

    This model4.451.851.32

    Single games are noisy: a 25-point scorer often scores 15 or 35. The model beats the usual averages, but no projection is a guarantee.

    What it doesn't know

    • Late scratches and minutes limits announced after the update.
    • Day-to-day players are assumed to play.
    • Coaching decisions, blowouts, and foul trouble.

    Projections are for learning and fun, not betting advice.
